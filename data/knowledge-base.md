## About
Sparkle Home Cleaning is a local cleaning service. We do homes and small offices, with vetted and insured cleaners.

## Services
- Standard clean: kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting, general tidy.
- Deep clean: everything in a standard clean plus inside the oven, inside the fridge, baseboards, and windows.
- Move-in or move-out clean: full deep clean for an empty property.
- Office clean: desks, shared areas, kitchen, and restrooms.

## Pricing
- Standard clean starts at $120 for a one-bedroom home.
- Deep clean starts at $220.
- Move-in or move-out starts at $300.
- Office cleans are quoted per visit after a short call.
Final price depends on size and condition. We confirm the quote before the visit.

## Service area
We cover the city center and suburbs within 20 miles. If you are outside that, ask and we will check.

## Hours
We clean Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. The office answers messages 7 days a week.

## Booking
To book, share your name, address, the type of clean, and a preferred day. We confirm a time slot and send a reminder the day before.

## Cancellation
Free to reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before the visit. Inside 24 hours we charge a small fee.

## Payment
We take card and bank transfer. Payment is due after the clean.
